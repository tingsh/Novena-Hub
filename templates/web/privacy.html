Privacy notice

How Novena expects to handle personal data across the public site and Hub. This draft requires legal and operational approval.

Prelaunch draft. Confirm the legal entity, data protection contact, processors, retention schedule, overseas transfers, and effective date before publication. Marketing analytics and inquiry measurement remain disabled pending that review.

Information collected

For demo requests, the site collects the name, work email, company, interest, and message submitted. Hub account creation and use may include account identifiers, team membership, role, authentication records, support requests, and service activity. Connected sites send equipment telemetry and Gateway status; some records may identify a user or site contact.

How information is used

Inquiry details are used to respond and prepare a relevant pilot conversation. Account and operational data are used to provide Hub access, process telemetry, display dashboards, route alerts, support customers, and protect the service. We do not use the public demo form to subscribe visitors to a marketing list.

Cookies and analytics

Essential session, authentication, CSRF, and preference cookies support site and Hub operation. Public marketing analytics and inquiry event tracking are disabled for this launch draft. If approved later, this notice and any required controls must be updated before they are enabled.

Service providers and transfers

Hosting, transactional email, payment processing, and authentication providers may process data as needed to provide contracted services. The final notice must name relevant providers or categories, locations, and safeguards for any overseas transfers.

Retention and security

Personal data should be kept only as needed for service, support, security, contractual, or legal purposes under an approved retention schedule. Access is restricted by account roles and operational controls. The final notice must reflect verified security and deletion practices.

Your choices and requests

For access, correction, withdrawal, or questions about personal data, contact Novena through the contact form and identify the account or inquiry involved. We will route the request to the responsible team once the formal data protection contact is confirmed.

Updates

The approved notice will include an effective date and a process for communicating material changes.
