Terms of service

This working draft is available for review. It is not the final agreement for a customer deployment.

Prelaunch draft. Legal counsel must approve the contracting entity, governing law, commercial terms, liability provisions, data processing terms, and effective date before public launch.

1. Service and scope

Novena Platform provides software for connecting supported equipment through gateways, viewing telemetry in Novena Hub, and using configured alerts and workflows. The exact subscription, enabled features, site scope, services, and support commitments will be stated in an order or pilot agreement.

2. Accounts and responsibilities

Account administrators are responsible for inviting appropriate users, keeping credentials secure, configuring site and device access, and ensuring that submitted information and equipment connections are authorised. Users must not interfere with service security or the equipment of others.

3. Equipment and connectivity

Gateway hardware, field installation, networks, and connectivity are separate from the platform subscription unless an order states otherwise. Customers remain responsible for site access and safe installation by qualified personnel. Remote control, where supported, requires a separately agreed safety and permission model.

4. Data and use

Customers retain responsibility for the equipment and account data they provide. Novena uses that data to deliver the service, support requested workflows, maintain security, and meet contractual obligations. Personal data handling is described in the privacy notice; customer data processing terms require separate legal review.

5. Plans, pilots, and payment

Public plan prices are indicative monthly platform estimates. Final fees, billing dates, taxes, device and Gateway limits, pilot scope, and any hardware or service charges will be agreed in writing before a paid deployment.

6. Availability and operational decisions

Telemetry, alerts, and integrations depend on field equipment, power, networks, and configuration. Operators should verify important readings and maintain appropriate site procedures. Any service levels or remedies must be specified in the applicable agreement.

7. Contact

Questions about a proposed deployment can be sent through the contact form.
